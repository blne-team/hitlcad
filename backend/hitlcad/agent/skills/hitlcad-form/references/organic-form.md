# Organic and figurative form

Use for a figurine, expressive shell or sculptural product. Keep exact interfaces distinct from the
freer outer surface. Read the chosen backend's code contract before implementing geometry.

## Establish gesture and masses

Identify the action or directional flow: leaning, sitting, reaching, twisting or tapering. Establish
primary masses around that gesture, then secondary forms, then fine detail. For a character, a few
landmarks such as head, muzzle, ears, paws and tail can expose proportional errors early.

Compare head/body size, mass spacing and the silhouette's recognisable features in several views.
Check that separate masses connect by real volume where they should form one body. Tangential
contact or a thin neck can look joined while being unsuitable for manufacture.

## Choose controls at the right level

Expose head tilt, ear flare, tail gesture, taper or bulge position where they support the brief.
Keep attachment depth and mating dimensions independent of expressive scaling. A larger head might
need a revised joint or support strategy; proportional scaling alone cannot establish that.

Prefer deliberate cross-sections and smooth transitions to a large pile of uncoordinated primitive
forms. Primitive masses are useful during exploration, but inspect the final silhouette for lumps,
pinches and accidental symmetry. Use detail to reinforce the main form rather than distract from it.

## Parting and manufacture

Choose cuts through sufficiently thick sections and, where possible, near natural boundaries such
as a collar or material transition. Avoid a plane grazing a curved surface into a feather edge.
Place alignment features where their installation and surrounding material can be inspected.

For Blender, preserve the backend contract for deterministic parameters, modifiers and manifold
geometry. Use enough resolution at a mating cylinder for the reported fit uncertainty. Smooth
shading changes appearance, not the underlying fit or wall thickness.

## Review

Inspect the back and top as well as the expected main view. Review wall thickness, joints, supports
or resin drains for the chosen process through hitlcad. For a parameterized object, vary a gesture
control and a proportion control independently to inspect what else changes.

Source for the visual hierarchy and proportional vocabulary:
[Getty's formal analysis resources](https://www.getty.edu/education/teachers/classroom_resources/formal_analysis.html).
The modeling and parting guidance also reflects the framework's Blender contract; it is not evidence
of a measured improvement in recognition or print success.
